Lognormal distribution
======================

Median from mean and standard deviation
---------------------------------------

mean_ln = ln(mean) - 1/2 * ln(1 + stdev^2 / mean^2)
median = exp(mean_ln) = mean / sqrt(1 + stedv^2 / mean^2)

stdev_ln^2 = ln(1 + stdev^2 / mean^2)


Mean from mean of logs and standard deviation of logs
-----------------------------------------------------

mean = exp(mean_ln + stdev_ln^2 / 2)
